Frequently asked questions

If you have any other questions, reach out from the Contact page.

How is the price determined?

After you upload your document, our team reviews it and prepares a personalized quote. The quote is sent by email, and no charge applies until you approve it.

How long does the translation take?

The AI draft is generated within seconds of upload. Review time and the quote timeline depend on the document's length.

How do I approve the quote?

Just click the "Accept" link in the quote email — no account needed.

Which languages do you support?

We currently offer translation between Turkish, English, and French.

What happens if I decline the quote?

Declining a quote never incurs a charge. You're welcome to submit a new request anytime.
